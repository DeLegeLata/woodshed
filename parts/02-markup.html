    Speed runs
    
      
    Fast pentatonic, Eric Johnson & Bonamassa style
    
      
    
        
    Speed comes from repeating small cells cleanly, not from forcing long runs. Every pattern here has its own working tempo. It starts slow, and each rep you log moves it: Nailed it pushes it up, Struggled pulls it back. Woodshed finds your speed for you.

Each click is one group, so the fretboard (with Guide on) lights up the notes that belong to that beat.
